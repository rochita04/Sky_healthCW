Completing Health Check

1. Each question will have a topic name at the top.

2. Use the buttons to select a state for each aspect:

  • Click on the "Green" button if the aspect is healthy.
  • Click on the "Yellow" button if there are some concerns.
  • Click on the "Red" button if the aspect needs urgent attention.

3. You can add comments to any of the 3 comment boxes provided.

4. Once you have made your selections, click next to continue to the next question.
